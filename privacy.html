Privacy Policy & Data Handling

How We Collect, Protect, and Manage Client and User Information

Our Data Commitment

At Handyman Painting L.L.C., we treat your personal information, project notes, and financial interactions with the same precision and security standards applied to our physical construction sites. We do not sell, trade, or monetize client data under any circumstances.

1. Information Collection

We collect contact details, property addresses, and project scope requirements solely for the purpose of generating accurate bids, scheduling service crews, and maintaining communication throughout project execution.

2. Secure Storage & Encryption

All digital records, timecards, and transaction data are stored locally and across encrypted cloud partitions utilizing AES-256-GCM encryption parameters and multi-factor authentication controls.

3. Third-Party Integrations

Where payment processing or secure verification is required (such as Stripe or Plaid financial gateways), data exchange is handled through secure, tokenized APIs that comply with rigorous federal financial protection standards.
