Independent Tiered Systems

Granular Tier Hierarchies for Residential, Commercial, and State/Federal Customer Groups & Field Crews

CUSTOMER GROUP 01

Residential Customer & Crew Tiers

Single-family homes, interior/exterior re-finishing, and property owner workflows.

RESIDENTIAL TIER 1

Standard Single-Room / Accent Repaint

Basic interior surface touch-ups, single room refreshing, and standard prep execution handled by Junior Field Technicians.

RESIDENTIAL TIER 2

Full Interior / Exterior Residence

Comprehensive whole-house painting, complete surface sanding, and multi-day crew execution led by an assigned Residential Crew Lead.

RESIDENTIAL TIER 3

Complex Restoration & Historic Property

Specialized historic woodwork restoration, extensive substrate stripping, custom staining, and direct oversight by Master Craftsmen.

CUSTOMER GROUP 02

Commercial Customer & Subcontractor Tiers

Apartment complexes, retail facilities, property management portfolios, and structural repairs.

COMMERCIAL TIER 1

Retail Turnkey & Maintenance

Single retail unit refreshes, door/hardware reinforcement, and fast-turnaround maintenance handled by vetted commercial technicians.

COMMERCIAL TIER 2

Multi-Family Apartment Complexes

Phased hallway painting, unit turnover packages, exterior siding maintenance, and specialized substrate repair protocols.

COMMERCIAL TIER 3

Large-Scale Industrial / Facility Asset

Full facility asset coatings, structural steel protection, heavy commercial scaffolding coordination, and multi-crew general contracting oversight.

CUSTOMER GROUP 03

State & Federal Customer & Compliance Tiers

Public sector procurement, state supplier portal execution, and regulated government contracting.

STATE TIER 1

Municipal & Local Public Property

STATE TIER 2

State Department & Agency Contracts

Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry compliant contracting (IR815804), prevailing wage alignment, and structured administrative reporting.

STATE TIER 3

Federal & Bonded Infrastructure Projects

Fully bonded federal/state public works, NIST SP 800-53 aligned secure data handling, multi-agency audit compliance, and enterprise executive management.
